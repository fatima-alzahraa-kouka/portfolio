02

Framing the problem

Problem statement

Each school had its own set of applications and portals, with different login platforms and links — so reports were written and analysed by hand, with a high risk of human error and wasted time.

Which we can solve by

providing one highly editable platform for all schools, with one-click login and a specially created interface for each login.

Personas

Three roles, three different days

Operator

Oversees the school network

Tracks schools, inspections and network-wide reports from a desk, across dozens of schools at once.

Pain Reports sit 6+ clicks deep and can’t be compared side by side.

School admin

Runs one school day to day

Handles staff, parent and teacher requests, and the timetable — constantly interrupted.

Pain Approvals are scattered across menus meant for other roles.

Teacher

Teaches, then catches up

Marks attendance, looks up students and messages parents — mostly between classes, often on a phone.

Pain No mobile view; finding one student record takes minutes.

05

Testing with real users

What we learned

Pinned from the test sessions

  • All roles found their home screen without help.

  • School admins wanted student KPIs for a specific time and class in one tap.

  • Operators wanted one-tap reports, with optional filters first.

  • Teachers wanted attendance in one tap.

06

Iterate & hand off

Iterations

What changed after testing

School admins wanted student KPIs for a specific time and class in one tap

→

Class and period filters added to the dashboard

Teachers wanted attendance in one tap

→

“Mark all present” by default, then edit exceptions

Operators wanted to generate reports in one tap, or set filters first and then generate

→

Default filters with more on demand — or build a new report and save it for later
